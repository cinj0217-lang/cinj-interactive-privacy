Privacy Policy for Toad Jump

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Toad Jump does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app does not require an internet connection and does not request internet access permissions. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no account or sign-in system.

The only information Toad Jump stores is saved locally on your own device:

  • Your chosen player name
  • Your best climb score and total games played
  • Total flies eaten

This information never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is automatically removed if you uninstall the app.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: chris2k360@gmail.com
